SLIDE TYPES & OPTIONS:

1. TEXT SLIDE

2. IMAGE SLIDE

   background: #000000

3. VIDEO SLIDE
   ## slide: video
   fit: fullscreen | inset | positioned
   background: #000000
   loop: true | false
   muted: true | false
   assets/your-video.mp4

FIT OPTIONS:
- fullscreen: Covers entire slide (no distortion, may crop)
- inset: Centered, max 90% of slide, maintains aspect ratio
- positioned: Original dimensions, scaled down if needed, centered

ASSETS:
- Place in assets/ folder relative to presentation
- Supports: jpg, png, webp, mp4, etc.
